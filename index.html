Why your appeal gets rejected on the first line

Amazon never tells you what signal it caught. It only tells you which policy you broke. Sellers answer the policy. Reviewers are looking for the signal.

01

You wrote a template

"We take full responsibility and have strengthened our internal processes." Every rejected appeal contains some version of this sentence. It describes nothing. It proves nothing. It is the fastest way to get skipped.

02

Your root cause is generic

"We were careless." That is not a root cause — it is an apology. A root cause names a specific action on a specific date: a supplier invoice dated after listing, an address that differs by one unit number, a VA who logged in from the wrong device.

03

Your evidence contradicts your story

The single most common structural failure: the explanation says one thing, the invoice says another. Reviewers cross-check dates and addresses. One mismatch and the whole submission is classified as unreliable.

Questions

Does this guarantee reinstatement?

No. Nobody can guarantee that, and anyone who does is lying to you. What this gives you is the same diagnostic process a professional appeal service runs — so your submission is judged on the merits of your case rather than on a writing error.

Is this only for Amazon?

The core logic (root cause → evidence → structure → self-audit) applies to Amazon, TikTok Shop, Walmart and Etsy. The templates are written for Amazon Seller Central and TikTok Shop Seller Center, with notes on where the other platforms differ.

What if I've already been rejected twice?

Module 6 is written specifically for that situation. The short version: appeal three cannot reuse any evidence from appeal one or two. The module covers the three legitimate ways to introduce new information, and when to stop and escalate instead.
